Greywater Ridge Wind Farm Operations Report

Greywater Ridge operates 47 turbines of model VX-167, each rated 4.3 MW,
for a total nameplate capacity of 202 MW. The 2025 availability was 96.8
percent and net generation reached 612 GWh. Blade inspections found leading
edge erosion on eleven turbines; repairs are scheduled for the autumn
maintenance window. The site manager is Priya Natarajan.
