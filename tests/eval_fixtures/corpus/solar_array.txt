Caldera Flats Solar Array Technical Specification

The Caldera Flats photovoltaic array comprises 612,000 bifacial modules with
a combined DC rating of 310 MW. The site occupies 1,150 hectares of former
grazing land. Single-axis trackers are supplied by Helios Mounting Systems.

Performance summary table:

| Metric                  | Value      |
|-------------------------|------------|
| DC capacity             | 310 MW     |
| AC capacity             | 255 MW     |
| Annual yield            | 710 GWh    |
| Performance ratio       | 84.2%      |
| Degradation (year 1)    | 1.9%       |

Grid connection is at the Pinyon substation via a 34 km, 230 kV line.
