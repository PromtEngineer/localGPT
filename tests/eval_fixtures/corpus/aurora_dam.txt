Aurora Dam Hydroelectric Feasibility Study

The Aurora Dam project on the Kestrel River proposes an installed capacity of
240 MW across three Francis turbines. The estimated capital cost is 1.85
billion dollars with first power targeted for March 2029. The reservoir will
inundate 14 square kilometres and the dam crest height is 96 metres.

The lead consultant is Meridian Hydro Partners and the environmental review
is chaired by Dr. Sofia Ramirez. Annual generation is projected at 1,120 GWh
with a capacity factor of 53 percent.
