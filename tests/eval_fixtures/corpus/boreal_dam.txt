Boreal Dam Hydroelectric Feasibility Study

The Boreal Dam project on the Tamarack River proposes an installed capacity
of 410 MW across four Kaplan turbines. The estimated capital cost is 2.6
billion dollars with first power targeted for August 2031. The dam crest
height is 71 metres and the reservoir covers 9 square kilometres.

The lead consultant is Northpeak Engineering and the environmental review is
chaired by Dr. Alan Whitfield. Annual generation is projected at 1,870 GWh
with a capacity factor of 52 percent.
