Saltmarsh Battery Energy Storage System Commissioning Summary

The Saltmarsh BESS provides 150 MW / 600 MWh using lithium iron phosphate
cells in 96 containerised units. Round-trip efficiency measured during
commissioning was 87.4 percent. The system passed grid-forming compliance
tests on 14 February 2026. The EPC contractor was Coastal Grid Solutions
and the warranty covers 7,000 equivalent full cycles.
