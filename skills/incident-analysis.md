---
name: Incident analysis
description: Root-cause framing for incidents and post-mortems.
version: 1
---

Frame the answer as a concise incident analysis.

- Lead with a one-line impact statement (what was affected, and how badly).
- Reconstruct a brief timeline of events in order, using only dated/sequenced facts from the sources.
- Separate "what happened" from "why it happened" (contributing factors vs. root cause).
- If the sources do not establish a root cause, say so plainly rather than speculating.
- End with concrete, source-supported remediation or follow-up items, if any are present.
