# health_docs benchmark — methodology notes

- **Every hop was verified by rendering the actual PDF page** (Read tool, absolute PDF page numbers, first page = 1) and reading the number off the page. No answer is taken from the PDF text layer alone, and none from memory. Figure values that sit between gridlines were re-checked by cropping the panel at 400-500 dpi (this corrected two first-pass misreads: hlt014 Fig. 1 infant peak is ~0.75/1,000, not ~0.95; hlt018 Fig. 1 primary-series plateau is ~67%, not ~63%).
- **Manifest page hints are unreliable — do not trust them as ground truth.** hlt012: Figure 1 is on PDF p.8 and Table 1 on p.9 (manifest says pp.6-7). hlt011: Tables 1-4 are on PDF pp.24-27 and the dosing-algorithm figure on p.7 (manifest says pp.9-10). Hints for hlt003, hlt015, hlt017 and hlt023 checked out.
- **Text layer is unusable for the FDA labels** (hlt020-hlt024): dosing and adverse-reaction tables extract as detached headers and loose whitespace, and Mounjaro's Table 1 extracts with no values at all. All drug-label numbers were read visually. This is deliberate — those questions are a genuine test of the multimodal path, and a text-only pipeline should fail them.
- **Figure hops are figure-only by construction.** Chosen because the value is *not* restated in body text: hlt003 Fig. 2 (rates by race/ethnicity), hlt012 Fig. 1 (weekly peaks), hlt014 Fig. 1 (weekly infant rates), hlt015 Fig. 1.9 (income-group ranks), hlt018 Fig. 1 (vaccination coverage). hlt002 Fig. 1 is paired with its table so the approximate read can be graded against 70.5%/88.3%.
- **Approximate figure reads** ("approximately 205 per 100,000", "roughly 0.75 per 1,000", "~67%") should be graded with tolerance — roughly +/-10% of the stated value, or the ranges given in the answer text. Every computed value states its inputs so a grader can check the arithmetic independently.
- **Docs deliberately not used for QA (7 of 24):** hlt016 (WHS 2025) restates the same Global Health Estimates 2021 figures as hlt015, so any cross-doc pair between them is degenerate — both simply agree. hlt019 (NCD Progress Monitor, 238 pp) is 200+ near-identical country profiles; retrieval cannot discriminate between them and the questions would grade luck. hlt004's Figure 1 percent-positive peaks (SARS-CoV-2 17.9%, influenza 31.6%, RSV 11.0%) are all restated verbatim in the body text, so it yields no true figure-only hop. hlt001, hlt008, hlt009 and hlt011 are sound but were cut to hold the set at 15 and keep the flu/COVID/RSV + vaccination + GLP-1 theme tight.
- **Coverage:** 17 of 24 docs, 33 hops (17 table / 6 figure / 10 text). 13 of 15 questions span 2+ documents; the 2 single-doc questions (hlt_q13, hlt_q14) are figure_read, where the difficulty is the chart, not the retrieval.
- **Known soft spot:** hlt_q07 compares RSV coverage among *all* nursing home residents (hlt013, 17.9%) with community adults aged >=75 (hlt010, 39.7%). The denominators are not age-matched; the question names both populations explicitly so the comparison stays gradable, but it is a population contrast, not a like-for-like rate difference.
